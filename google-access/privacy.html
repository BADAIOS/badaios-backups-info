Privacy policy - BADAIOS Google Access

BADAIOS Google Access

Effective September 24, 2026.

Purpose and information accessed

BADAIOS Google Access is a private configuration operated by its owner, not a public service. Google Workspace MCP provides the Google connection. Interactive Pi sessions use Pi MCP Adapter, and interactive Claude Code sessions use native MCP support.

The owner authorizes each account through Google OAuth. Requested permissions cover Google account identity, Gmail, Google Drive, and Google Calendar. These permissions are broader than access to files created by the app. Depending on the task and granted permissions, accessed information can include account identifiers, email headers and bodies, attachments, labels and filters, file names and contents, sharing metadata, calendars, events, and attendee details.

Use and changes

The connection uses Google information to perform the owner's requested tasks, such as finding correspondence, reading documents, preparing responses, organizing files, or managing a calendar. Its tools can also send email, change messages or settings, create or change files, change sharing permissions, and create, update, or delete calendar events. The owner must confirm sends, sharing changes, deletion, and other consequential actions.

Only the owner's interactive sessions are authorized to use the Google connection. Delegated subagents have no Google access grant under this configuration. Tool-call restrictions do not provide operating-system credential isolation.

AI processing and sharing

Google information selected for a task may be sent to the AI service used by the interactive client, including OpenAI or Anthropic. That service processes the request under its applicable terms and account settings. Its retention and processing are separate from local storage. The owner must use service settings and agreements compatible with Google's Limited Use requirements before transmitting Google information.

The owner does not authorize sale of Google user data, use for advertising, or training of generalized AI models with that data. Information is used for the requested personal productivity task. Sending a message, inviting an attendee, or sharing a file can disclose information to the recipients the owner approves. Google processes information under its privacy policy.

Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs must follow the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Local storage and credentials

The connector initially uses its supported local OAuth credential storage with restricted file permissions. Credentials authorize requests to Google and are not intended as assistant input. OAuth secrets and tokens must not be published, pasted into prompts, or copied into public records. This local storage is not a claim of a separate credential broker or operating-system isolation.

Requested messages, documents, attachments, and results may remain in local downloads, assistant conversation histories, or the owner's private project and knowledge records. Operational records may contain account identifiers, file names, timestamps, requested operations, and success or failure information. Those records are not published on this website.

Retention, access, and deletion

The owner controls local records and retention. There is no automatic deletion deadline promised by this configuration. Retained copies and backups may remain until the owner removes them under the relevant retention procedure. The selected AI service's retention also depends on its terms and account settings.

The owner can revoke access through the Google account's third-party connections settings. Revocation stops future requests that require that grant. It does not undo earlier sends or sharing changes, delete Google files or events, or erase copies already retained locally or by an AI service. Those require separate action.

This website and contact

This static website has no sign-in forms, upload forms, advertising, or analytics code. It receives no Google messages, files, calendar contents, or OAuth credentials from the connector. Its hosting provider may retain ordinary web-request logs.

For questions about a private installation, its information, or deletion, contact the account owner who configured it. General website issues may be reported through the website repository. Do not submit private data or credentials there.
