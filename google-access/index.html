BADAIOS Google Access

A private, owner-operated connection between the owner's Google accounts and interactive AI assistants. It uses Google Workspace MCP with Pi MCP Adapter or Claude Code's native MCP support.

The owner authorizes each Google account through Google's consent screen. Depending on the permissions granted, the connection can search and read Gmail messages and attachments, prepare drafts and send messages, read and change Drive files and sharing settings, and read and manage Calendar events.

Google content requested for a task may enter the interactive assistant's context and be processed by its selected AI service. The owner controls the requests and must confirm sends, sharing changes, deletion, and other consequential actions. Delegated subagents have no Google access grant under this configuration.

This website only describes the connection. It has no Google sign-in flow, public registration, upload form, or access to connected accounts. The connection is being configured and verified; this page is not a claim that a particular account is connected.

Privacy policy

For questions about a private installation or its data, contact the account owner who configured it. General website issues can be reported through the website repository. Do not post credentials, messages, documents, or other private information there.
