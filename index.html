BADAIOS Backups

A private, owner-operated backup configuration using rclone, local archive encryption, and Google Drive.

The configuration creates encrypted archives on the owner's computer and uses an authorized Google account to upload, list, verify, and retrieve those archives. The owner controls the destination account and retention.

This is an information page, not a public backup service. There are no public registrations, file uploads, or sign-in forms on this website.

Privacy policy

For questions about an installed configuration, contact the account owner who configured it. General website issues can be reported through the website repository; do not post credentials or private information there.
