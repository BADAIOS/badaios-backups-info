BADAIOS Backups

Privacy policy

Effective September 10, 2026.

Purpose and Google Drive access

BADAIOS Backups is a private configuration operated by its owner, not a public service. It uses rclone on the owner's computer to upload, list, verify, and retrieve owner-selected encrypted backups in Google Drive.

This configuration requests Google's drive.file permission, not unrestricted Google Drive access. That permission covers files created with the app or explicitly selected for use with it. In this rclone configuration, the app creates and manages its own backup files. Older archives made by a different client are not automatically accessible; the owner can retrieve those separately through their Google Drive account.

Storage and protection

Archive contents are encrypted locally before upload. Google Drive stores the encrypted archives and associated backup metadata. Encryption recovery material is kept separately from the uploaded archives. OAuth credentials are stored locally with restricted file permissions and used to authorize requests to Google's authentication and Drive services.

Operational records may contain file names, archive sizes, checksums, timestamps, account identifiers, and success or failure information. Those records are retained locally or in the owner's private operational records, not published on this website.

Use and sharing

Google user data is used only to provide and verify the owner's backup and restore operations. It is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train generalized AI models. No backup contents or OAuth credentials are sent to this website. Google processes data under its own privacy policy.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention, access, and deletion

The owner controls archive retention and can inspect, download, or delete backups through Google Drive. Existing protected snapshots are not removed automatically. Backup schedules and local records remain under the owner's control.

Access can be revoked through the Google account's third-party connections settings. Revoking access stops future authorized requests; it does not automatically erase archives already stored in Drive. The owner can also remove local OAuth credentials and operational records when they are no longer required.

This website and contact

This static website has no sign-in forms, upload forms, advertising, or analytics code. Its hosting provider may retain ordinary web-request logs. Do not submit private data or credentials to the website repository.

For help with a private installation or its data, contact the account owner who configured it. General website issues may be reported through the website repository.
